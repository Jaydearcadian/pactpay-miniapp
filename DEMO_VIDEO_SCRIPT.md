# PactPay — Demo Video Script

**Target runtime:** 2 minutes 20 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9, 1440p or 1080p, 30 fps  
**Voice:** calm, assured, technically literate, warm; medium-low register; 135–145 words per minute  
**Performance direction:** speak with deliberate confidence, not advertising hype. Pause briefly after the opening problem, after “one private contribution room,” and before the final sentence. Emphasize *clear terms*, *role-scoped*, *evidence*, *NIM*, and *settled*. Pronounce Nimiq as **“nee-meek”** and NIM as **“nim.”**

---

## 00:00–00:08 — Cold open

**Screen**  
Fade from black into the PactPay landing page. Begin with a slow 4% push toward the hero statement. Keep the cursor hidden.

**Voiceover**  
> Collaborative work usually breaks at the same point: the outcome is shared, but the obligations, evidence, and payments are not.

**On-screen title**  
`PACTPAY`

**On-screen subtitle**  
`Private contributions. Clear evidence. Direct NIM settlement.`

---

## 00:08–00:22 — Product definition

**Screen**  
Scroll slowly through the landing page. Hold briefly on the decisive workflow and the Nimiq Pay explanation. Cut to the application workspace.

**Voiceover**  
> PactPay turns one shared outcome into independently managed private contributions. A coordinator defines the work. Each contributor receives only their own role, terms, deadlines, and entitlement. Accepted work is settled directly through Nimiq Pay.

---

## 00:22–00:40 — Create the outcome

**Screen**  
Click **Create an outcome**. Enter:

- Outcome name: `Launch the PactPay competition demo`
- Contributor-visible alias: `Mini App launch programme`
- Target date: a valid future date

Submit and allow the success state to remain visible for one second.

**Voiceover**  
> The coordinator starts with the outcome. The public-facing alias keeps the broader client context private, while the workspace retains the coordination view needed to manage delivery.

**Editing note**  
Use a clean jump cut between completed form fields. Do not show slow typing for every character.

---

## 00:40–01:00 — Define one private contribution

**Screen**  
Create a contribution with:

- Contributor role: `Demo editor`
- Entitlement: `0.01 NIM`
- Deliverable: `A final two-minute PactPay product demo`
- Acceptance criteria:
  - `Shows the complete coordinator and contributor workflow`
  - `Includes a readable settlement receipt`
  - `Delivered as a public video link`
- Valid acceptance and delivery deadlines

Submit the form. Hold on the contribution card and its terms fingerprint state.

**Voiceover**  
> Each contribution is defined as a precise deal: the role, obligation, acceptance criteria, entitlement, and deadlines. PactPay generates a deterministic terms fingerprint, creating a stable reference for the private handoff and the returned evidence.

---

## 01:00–01:16 — Share the invitation

**Screen**  
Click **Share private invitation**. Show the compact confirmation. Then click **Open private invitation** to reveal the new branded PactPay invitation dialog. Paste the copied link and open the contribution room.

**Voiceover**  
> The coordinator shares one private invitation. The handoff contains only this contribution. It does not expose the other contributors, the full outcome ledger, or unrelated commercial context.

**Editing note**  
Make the branded invitation dialog the visual focus. Use a subtle 105% zoom for two seconds. Do not show any browser-native prompt.

---

## 01:16–01:35 — Contributor acceptance in Nimiq Pay

**Screen**  
Switch to a mobile recording inside Nimiq Pay. Show the contribution room at full device width. Scroll through the obligation, acceptance criteria, entitlement, and deadlines. Tap **Connect Nimiq and accept** and approve the native account prompt.

**Voiceover**  
> Inside Nimiq Pay, the contributor sees a focused contribution room. They review the exact terms, connect their Nimiq account, and accept through the wallet’s native confirmation flow. PactPay never receives the private key.

**Capture requirement**  
Use a real mobile capture or a correctly framed 390-by-844 device viewport. Text must remain legible without zooming the final video.

---

## 01:35–01:50 — Submit evidence

**Screen**  
Paste a public evidence URL and enter a short note: `Final video, source recording, and review notes included.` Tap **Submit contribution**. Share or copy the response link.

**Voiceover**  
> When the work is complete, the contributor submits an evidence link and note. PactPay fingerprints that evidence together with the contribution context, then returns a private response to the coordinator.

---

## 01:50–02:08 — Review and settle

**Screen**  
Return to the coordinator view. Open the response. Hold on the matched contribution and evidence fingerprint. Click **Approve and pay 0.01 NIM**. Show the native Nimiq Pay payment confirmation and approve it.

**Voiceover**  
> The coordinator receives the response, verifies that it matches the original contribution, reviews the evidence, and approves payment. Nimiq Pay displays the recipient and amount before broadcasting the NIM transaction.

**Audio direction**  
Lower the music by 4 decibels during the wallet confirmation. Let the native confirmation sound remain audible if it is clean.

---

## 02:08–02:20 — Receipt and close

**Screen**  
Reveal the final receipt. Hold long enough to read:

- `0.01 NIM sent`
- recipient
- evidence fingerprint
- transaction hash
- settled time

Then cut to the landing hero and logo.

**Voiceover**  
> The result is a verifiable receipt linking the accepted evidence, recipient, transaction hash, amount, and settlement time. One outcome. Private contributions. Everyone settled. That is PactPay.

**Final card**  
`PACTPAY`  
`Clear terms. Private fulfilment. Verifiable NIM settlement.`

---

## Voice-generation prompt

Use this exact direction with the selected high-quality text-to-speech voice:

> Read as a calm product architect presenting a working financial application to technical judges. Use a warm, medium-low voice with precise diction and restrained confidence. Maintain approximately 140 words per minute. Avoid exaggerated enthusiasm, radio-announcer energy, or robotic cadence. Use natural sentence-level phrasing, short pauses after important claims, and a slightly slower pace when explaining privacy, evidence fingerprints, Nimiq Pay confirmation, and the final settlement receipt. Pronounce “Nimiq” as “nee-meek,” “NIM” as “nim,” and “PactPay” as “Pact Pay.”

## Recording and edit quality gate

The video is ready only when all of the following are true:

- The first product action appears within 22 seconds.
- Every spoken claim is visibly demonstrated.
- No browser-native prompt, broken link, loading failure, console, terminal, test key, or secret appears.
- The mobile contribution room is legible at normal playback size.
- Cursor movement is deliberate and clicks are easy to follow.
- The invitation, response, and settlement transitions are shown in the correct order.
- The native Nimiq Pay account and payment approvals are visible.
- The final receipt remains on screen for at least five seconds.
- Background music never competes with narration.
- The narration peaks below clipping and remains consistent across all scenes.
