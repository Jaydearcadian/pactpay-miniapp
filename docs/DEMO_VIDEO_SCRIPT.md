# PactPay — Competition Demo Video Script

**Target duration:** 1 minute 35 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 60 fps  
**Narration style:** premium product documentary; calm, precise, assured  
**Pronunciation:** PactPay = “Pact Pay”; Nimiq = “NIM-ik”; NIM = “nim”

## Voice direction

Use one warm, neutral adult voice with a lightly textured lower-mid register. The delivery must feel trustworthy and composed—not promotional, breathless, or theatrical.

- Pace: **0.93–0.96× normal conversational speed**
- Energy: **measured confidence**
- Pitch: natural; do not artificially deepen it
- Pauses: honour every em dash and paragraph break
- Emphasis: stress only the words shown in **bold**
- Avoid: upward inflection at sentence endings, exaggerated smiles, “startup” excitement, and heavy vocal fry
- Record dry and close, then apply light cleanup only
- Final mix: **48 kHz**, **-16 LUFS integrated**, **-1 dBTP ceiling**, gentle 2:1 compression, subtle de-essing, no audible reverb
- Music: optional low, restrained pulse at least **18 dB beneath the narration**; remove it entirely during wallet confirmation

---

## 00:00–00:08 — The coordination problem

### Screen
Start on a near-black frame. Fade into the PactPay landing page. Slowly track across the headline, then rest on **Create an outcome**.

### Voiceover
> A shared outcome can depend on many people—yet every contributor should not have to see the entire deal.

### Edit
Use a 12-frame fade. No cursor movement until the final word.

---

## 00:08–00:18 — Product truth

### Screen
Click **Open app**. Show the coordinator workspace in its empty state. Move the cursor once, deliberately, toward **Create an outcome**.

### Voiceover
> **PactPay** is a private contribution ledger for defining work clearly, sharing only what each contributor needs, and settling accepted work in NIM.

### Edit
On-screen title, lower left:

**PACTPAY**  
_Private outcome settlement_

Keep it visible for four seconds, then fade.

---

## 00:18–00:31 — Create the outcome

### Screen
Create an outcome named **Summer product launch**. Use the contributor-visible alias **Product launch programme**. Select the target date and submit.

### Voiceover
> The coordinator starts with one outcome. The internal name stays in the coordinator workspace, while contributors receive only a carefully scoped alias.

### Edit
Do not accelerate typing beyond 1.4×. Cut directly after the outcome appears in the ledger.

---

## 00:31–00:47 — Define one private contribution

### Screen
Click **Add contribution** and enter:

- Role: **Launch designer**
- Entitlement: **0.01 NIM**
- Deliverable: **Final responsive launch page and editable source files**
- Acceptance criteria: **Desktop approved**, **Mobile approved**, **Editable source included**
- Set acceptance and delivery deadlines
- Create the invitation

### Voiceover
> Each contribution becomes a clear pact: the role, the obligation, the acceptance criteria, the entitlement, and both deadlines. PactPay fingerprints those terms and prepares a private invitation.

### Edit
Briefly highlight the entitlement and deadline fields. Do not zoom into every input.

---

## 00:47–00:58 — Private handoff

### Screen
Click **Share private invitation**. Show the compact confirmation. Then cut to the contributor device opening the shared link. Reveal the contribution sheet from top to bottom.

### Voiceover
> The contributor opens a role-scoped room. They see their obligation, their deadlines, and exactly what they will receive—without being exposed to the rest of the outcome.

### Edit
Use a clean match cut between devices. Keep the contributor sheet fully readable for at least three seconds.

---

## 00:58–01:11 — Acceptance and evidence

### Screen
Tap **Connect Nimiq and accept** inside Nimiq Pay. Show the native account confirmation briefly. Return to PactPay, paste the evidence link, add a short note, and submit. Share the response back to the coordinator.

### Voiceover
> Acceptance is tied to the contributor’s Nimiq account. When the work is ready, the contributor submits evidence and returns a private response to the coordinator.

### Edit
Lower or remove music during the native wallet confirmation. Never crop the approval context so tightly that it looks simulated.

---

## 01:11–01:25 — Review and settlement

### Screen
Open the response on the coordinator device. Show the matched terms, evidence link, contributor address, and **Approve and pay 0.01 NIM**. Tap it and show the Nimiq Pay payment confirmation.

### Voiceover
> The coordinator reviews the returned terms and evidence, then approves settlement. The payment is confirmed natively in Nimiq Pay—so PactPay never handles the user’s private keys.

### Edit
Hold the confirmation screen long enough to read the recipient and amount. Do not speed through the wallet approval.

---

## 01:25–01:35 — Receipt and close

### Screen
Reveal the PactPay settlement receipt: amount, recipient, evidence fingerprint, transaction hash, and settlement time. End on the landing-page wordmark and stable URL.

### Voiceover
> One outcome. Private contributions. Everyone settled. **PactPay—clear coordination, verifiable NIM settlement.**

### End card

**PactPay**  
One outcome. Private contributions. Everyone settled in NIM.  
`pactpay-miniapp.pages.dev`

Hold for three seconds. Fade to black.
